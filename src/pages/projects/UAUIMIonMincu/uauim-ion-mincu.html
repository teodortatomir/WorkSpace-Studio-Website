Ion Mincu Faculty of Architecture and Urbanism Interior design for the faculty’s main hall

SCOPE
Timeline

Completed December 2017

Location

Main hall, Ion Mincu Faculty of Architecture and Urbanism, Bucharest

The project concerned the main hall of the Ion Mincu Faculty of Architecture and Urbanism in Bucharest.

Justin Baroncea was the architect, and the scope included concept design.

THE OUTCOME

The project was completed in December 2017 and included Herman Miller products.

THE DIFFERENTIATION

Custom decorative elements were part of the main hall package.
