NESTLÉ 2021

NESTLÉ ROMANIA An Ergonomic, Fully Integrated Workplace for a Global Leader

SCOPE
Client

Nestlé Romania, global FMCG & nutrition company, large enterprise

Timeline

Completed January 2021

Budget

Not disclosed

Delivered

2,500 m² in BOC Office Building, Bucharest

Key constraint

Integrating multiple functions and suppliers into a single coherent workspace while maintaining ergonomic standards across all teams

Nestlé needed a modern office that could support a wide range of workstyles—from focused individual tasks to collaboration and relaxation—within a large, multifunctional floorplate. The challenge was to ensure ergonomic quality at scale while coordinating multiple suppliers and maintaining a consistent design language across all areas. The client placed strong emphasis on employee wellbeing and productivity, requiring a workspace that could meet high global standards.

The core idea was to build an ergonomic, human-centered environment using premium furniture and acoustic solutions. All individual workstations were equipped with Herman Miller Ratio height-adjustable desks, ensuring healthy posture and flexibility for every employee.

Acoustic comfort was addressed through Framery booths for private calls and Baux/Johanson Decibel wall treatments in meeting rooms. Upholstered furniture from HAY added warmth and visual coherence.

Because the project required a wide variety of functions and finishes, Workspace Studio coordinated multiple suppliers and managed the entire implementation process to ensure seamless integration and consistent quality.

Unlike standard fitouts that prioritize speed or uniformity, this approach focused on ergonomics, acoustic performance, and tailored solutions, creating a workspace aligned with Nestlé’s global standards of wellbeing and productivity.

THE OUTCOME
  • High employee comfort due to ergonomic workstations and acoustic solutions
  • Smooth delivery through coordinated multisupplier management
  • Strong client satisfaction, with Nestlé recognizing the impact of ergonomic design on productivity
  • A complete, multifunctional workspace supporting individual work, collaboration, and relaxation in a unified environment
THE DIFFERENTIATION

A typical competitor might have delivered a standard corporate layout with generic furniture and minimal acoustic treatment. Workspace Studio instead created a fully integrated, ergonomically optimized environment, leveraging premium suppliers and meticulous project coordination—resulting in a workspace that feels both high-performance and human-centered.
