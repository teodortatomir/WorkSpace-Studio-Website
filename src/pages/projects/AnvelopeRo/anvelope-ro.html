Anvelope.ro A customer view into a tyre workshop

SCOPE
Timeline

Completed December 2022

Area

340 m²

Location

Pipera

The project combined an automated tyre workshop with a customer reception and waiting area.

Mihai Barbu and Justin Baroncea designed the interior around movable elements. Workspace Studio supplied standard furniture, Atelier SET provided custom furniture, La Fereastra supplied the curtains, and Almalux provided the lighting systems.

THE OUTCOME

Customers can watch the hydraulic lifts and tyre-changing robots from the waiting area. Orange epoxy markings identify public routes on the ground floor.

THE DIFFERENTIATION

Wheeled steel frames furnish the reception, and orange velvet curtains divide the public area from the machinery. Movable glass walls and grey curtains allow the layout upstairs to be reconfigured.
