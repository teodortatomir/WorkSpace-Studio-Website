BAYER

Bayer A fit-out for a six-week relocation window

SCOPE
Timeline

Completed April 2019

Area

1,000 m²

Bayer was preparing to move into a new 1,000 m² office in six weeks.

Atelier 507 was the architect. Workspace Studio’s turnkey scope included fit-out, mechanical, electrical and plumbing works, office and custom-made furniture, decorative wall coverings, branding and signage.

THE OUTCOME

The furnishing and signage package covered 150 workstations, eight meeting rooms, relaxation areas and coffee points.

THE DIFFERENTIATION

Project and cost management were included alongside the fit-out and furnishing works.
