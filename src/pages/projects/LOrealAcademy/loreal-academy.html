L’ORÉAL ACADEMY

L’Oréal Academy Design and furnishing at U•Center 2

SCOPE
Timeline

Completed March 2025

Area

250 m²

Location

U•Center 2

The L’Oréal Academy project covered a 250 m² interior at U•Center 2.

Allspace Interiors, a division of Workspace Studio, designed the interior and provided the standard and custom-made furniture, flooring, branding, and acoustic and architectural solutions.

Forte Partners was the general contractor, and PM Solutions provided project management.

THE OUTCOME

The completed package included standard and custom-made furniture, flooring, and acoustic and architectural solutions.

THE DIFFERENTIATION

Allspace Interiors handled branding as part of its interior design and furnishing scope.
