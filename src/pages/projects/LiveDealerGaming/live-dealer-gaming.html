GLOBAL LIVE DEALER GAMING COMPANY

Global Live Dealer Gaming Company Furniture and gaming-themed acoustics

SCOPE
Timeline

Completed February 2023

Area

1,000 m²

Location

Globalworth Campus C, Dimitrie Pompeiu Boulevard, Bucharest

The office was designed in accordance with the company’s brand manual and included settings for teamwork, brainstorming and breaks.

SelfDezign led the interior architecture and design. Workspace Studio provided standard and custom-made furniture and acoustic solutions.

THE OUTCOME

Sofas, armchairs and poufs furnish the informal areas. The interior uses grey and beige with red and green accents.

THE DIFFERENTIATION

Gaming-themed wallpaper and acoustic cladding with motifs drawn from playing cards or dice carry the visual identity into the office.
