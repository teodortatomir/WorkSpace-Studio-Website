PROFI ROM FOOD

Profi Rom Food A headquarters designed for interaction

SCOPE
Timeline

Completed August 2023

Area

3,000 m²

Location

Bucharest

Profi wanted a headquarters that reflected the company’s identity and supported collaboration and flexible work.

Workspace Studio developed the architecture and interior design with Profi. Generous areas with natural light were designed to give employees places to interact.

THE OUTCOME

The project included standard and custom-made furniture, lighting, and acoustic and architectural solutions.

THE DIFFERENTIATION

The interior pairs technological features with warm, natural elements.
