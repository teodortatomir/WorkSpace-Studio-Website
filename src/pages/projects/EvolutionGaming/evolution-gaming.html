SCOPE
Timeline

May to December 2024

Area

1,000 m²

Location

9–9A Dimitrie Pompeiu Boulevard, Bucharest 020335

The Evolution Gaming project covered a 1,000 m² office interior on Dimitrie Pompeiu Boulevard in Bucharest.

The fit-out and furniture scope included architectural finishes and building services, including turnstiles and fingerprint readers.

THE OUTCOME

The project included two server rooms and CCTV monitoring.

THE DIFFERENTIATION

Lighting was dimmable and programmable throughout the office. The central-battery emergency lighting had an addressable option.
