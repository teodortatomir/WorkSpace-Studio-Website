Evolution Gaming- How do you deliver a 1,000 sqm office where access control, safety, and programmable lighting operate within the same performance logic?

SCOPE
Client

Evolution Gaming

Timeline

May 2024 - December 2024

Budget

Not publicly disclosed

Delivered

1,000 sqm, fit-out and furniture

Key constraint

Integrating advanced technical requirements - access control, safety systems, and dimmable/programmable lighting - into a cohesive fit-out delivery

Evolution Gaming required an office environment able to support not only day-to-day operations, but also a higher level of control, reliability, and technical performance. The project included elements such as turnstiles, fingerprint readers, centralized battery emergency lighting with addressable options, and dimmable, programmable lighting systems - all of which had to be incorporated into a coherent workplace, rather than appearing as fragmented technical add-ons.

Workspace Studio delivered the project as an integrated fit-out and furnishing programme, with close coordination between infrastructure, technology, suppliers, and the final workplace implementation.

The delivery and coordination logic focused on making technical systems highly effective without allowing them to dominate the user experience. Access systems, lighting scenarios, and safety components were treated as active parts of the workplace ecosystem. This created an office able to address security, comfort, and operational clarity at the same time.

Unlike a standard office fit-out, spatial performance here was not left solely to furniture or finishes. Instead, performance was embedded throughout the workplace system - from entry control and emergency response to lighting flexibility.

THE OUTCOME
  • Utilization/adoption metric: Not publicly disclosed
  • Timeline/budget performance: Project delivered between May and December 2024

THE DIFFERENTIATION

A competitor might have treated special systems - access control, programmable lighting, and safety infrastructure - as separate technical packages layered onto the interior fit-out. Workspace Studio coordinated and integrated them within the delivery flow, allowing the final space to function as a unified whole. In a workplace where technical performance is critical, this integration directly improves the quality and coherence of delivery.
