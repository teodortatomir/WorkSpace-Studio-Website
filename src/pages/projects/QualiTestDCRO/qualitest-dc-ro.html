QUALITEST DC RO 2022

Qualitest DC RO 2022 Expansion at Globalworth Square

SCOPE
Timeline

Completed May 2022

Area

2,500 m²

Location

Globalworth Square

Qualitest needed a larger office in 2022 and aimed to double its existing office area. The new space was also intended to encourage employees to return to the office.

Workspace Studio designed and implemented the turnkey project. Grey IVC carpet tiles and an industrial ceiling form the neutral backdrop.

THE OUTCOME

The shared areas include three cafeterias, relaxation areas and an outdoor terrace.

THE DIFFERENTIATION

Colour accents identify different zones and help people navigate the office.
