HBO ROMANIA

HBO Romania Office refurbishment at Premium Point

SCOPE
Timeline

Completed February 2021

Location

Premium Point, Bucharest

The refurbishment had to make HBO’s existing office more flexible and adaptable to the work of the local team.

1722 Studiolab Inc led the architecture, and Workspace Studio was the general contractor. Exposed industrial ceilings, subtle lighting, timber and raw steel were paired with furniture that gives the space a residential character.

THE OUTCOME

The refurbishment at Premium Point was completed in February 2021.

THE DIFFERENTIATION

The workstation specification combined Herman Miller Aeron chairs and electrically adjustable Ratio desks.
