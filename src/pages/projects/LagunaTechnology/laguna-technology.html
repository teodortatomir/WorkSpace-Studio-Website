LAGUNA TECHNOLOGY

Laguna Technology Ergonomic furniture for a relocation

SCOPE
Timeline

Completed July 2021

Area

400 m²

Location

Business Forum, Pitești

Laguna Technology was relocating its office to Business Forum in Pitești.

Criquet Architecture designed the interior. Workspace Studio carried out the turnkey project.

THE OUTCOME

The package included furniture, flooring and architectural elements.

THE DIFFERENTIATION

The relocation was used to bring ergonomic furnishings and a contemporary aesthetic into the workplace.
