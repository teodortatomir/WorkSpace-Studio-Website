TECHNOLOGY COMPANY 2018

COMPANIE DE TEHNOLOGIE A Contemporary Workspace Built for Agility and Growth

SCOPE
Client

Technology company (confidential name), technology sector, mid-sized

Timeline

Finalized October 2018

Budget

Not disclosed

Delivered

Full office fitout (architecture + office furniture)

Key constraint

Transforming a standard office floor into a flexible, future-proof environment within a tight delivery window

The client, a fast-growing technology company, needed a workspace that could support rapid team expansion, hybrid workflows, and a culture built on collaboration and innovation. Their existing environment lacked ergonomic structure, acoustic comfort, and the spatial logic required for a modern tech operation. The challenge was to design a space that balanced openness with focus, and identity with neutrality—while ensuring the project could be delivered efficiently and with minimal operational disruption.

The architectural concept, developed by Studio 3 Plus / arh. Dragoș Scurtulescu, focused on clarity, modularity, and a clean material palette that supports both productivity and brand expression. Workspace Studio provided all office furniture solutions, selecting ergonomic systems that allow teams to reconfigure quickly as projects evolve.

The design strategy emphasized:

  • Flexible open areas for teamwork and sprint sessions
  • Enclosed meeting rooms with controlled acoustics
  • Neutral, technology-friendly finishes that allow the space to evolve with the company
  • Efficient circulation to support fast-paced workflows

Unlike standard fitouts that prioritize density, this project prioritized agility and long-term adaptability, ensuring the space could scale with the company’s ambitions.

THE OUTCOME
  • A modern, ergonomic workspace aligned with the needs of a technology-driven team
  • Smooth delivery within the planned timeline (October 2018)
  • A functional environment that supports both collaboration and deep-focus work
  • A durable interior that continues to serve the company’s operational growth
THE DIFFERENTIATION

Where a typical competitor might have delivered a generic tech office with off-the-shelf solutions, this project combined architectural precision with tailored furniture integration, resulting in a workspace that is both highly functional and strategically future-ready. The collaboration between Studio 3 Plus and Workspace Studio ensured a coherent, high-quality outcome that stands out in its category.
