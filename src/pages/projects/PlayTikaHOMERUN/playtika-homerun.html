The Homerun Ciero entry covers a detail and build project.

The gallery credits Leitersdorf Design & Make Over and architects Dannah Leitersdorf and Elad Cohen. The scope covered turnkey construction and building-services installations.

THE OUTCOME

The package included office and custom-made furniture. The gallery lists completion dates in October 2014, August 2015 and June 2016.

THE DIFFERENTIATION

Parquet and carpet flooring were listed, along with partitions, signage and blinds.
