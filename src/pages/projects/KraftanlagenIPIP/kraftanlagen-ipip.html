KRAFTANLAGEN AND IPIP

Kraftanlagen and IPIP Industrial design for work and collaboration

SCOPE
Timeline

Completed January 2022

Area

2,800 m²

Location

LBC Building, Ploiești

The LBC Building office interiors were to be tailored to the investor’s needs.

Workspace Studio designed and built the interior. Concrete, wood, metal and glass sit alongside exposed ceilings and strong colour accents.

THE OUTCOME

The layout provides places for stand-up meetings, open booths, lounge areas and a plaza area. Herman Miller chairs and tools form part of the ergonomic specification.

THE DIFFERENTIATION

Tubular lighting and furniture frames draw on the forms of industrial piping and equipment.
