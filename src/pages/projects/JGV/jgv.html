JGV & ASOCIAȚII 2021

JGV & ASOCIAȚII A Contemporary Legal Workspace with a Distinct Identity

SCOPE
Client

JGV & Asociații, law firm, medium-sized

Timeline

Completed February 2021

Budget

Not disclosed

Delivered

600 m² in SPLAY Office Building, Bucharest

Key constraint

Creating a nontraditional legal workspace that aligns with a dynamic, collaborative workstyle

JGV & Asociații wanted to break away from the conventional, conservative aesthetic typical of law firms. Their ambition was to create a workspace that reflected their dynamic, collaborative, and fluid way of working, while also expressing a strong, recognizable identity. The challenge was to reinterpret the building’s Art Nouveau architecture into a contemporary interior that still felt warm, familiar, and aligned with the firm’s brand.

The design concept began with the relationship between the building’s Art Nouveau character and a curated palette of materials, textures, colors, and graphic elements. This interplay became the foundation of the interior identity.

The architectural composition drew from the firm’s corporate color palette, but applied it in unexpected, versatile, and durable ways to avoid the rigidity of traditional legal offices.

Carrara marble was introduced as a signature accent—on both walls and furniture—giving the space a unique, premium identity. Elegant terrace curtains, layered perspectives, and refined detailing created a warm, familiar atmosphere that balanced sophistication with approachability.

Rich materials, expressive flooring, and rounded forms contrasted with the building’s simple architectural lines, resulting in a space that feels both contemporary and timeless.

THE OUTCOME
  • A distinctive legal workspace that breaks away from traditional norms
  • Strong alignment between brand identity and spatial experience
  • A warm, premium atmosphere appreciated by both employees and clients
  • A long-term interior identity built on durable materials and thoughtful detailing
THE DIFFERENTIATION

Where a typical competitor might have delivered a conservative, wood-heavy legal office, this project embraced architectural reinterpretation and material expressiveness. The result is a workspace that feels modern, fluid, and unmistakably tied to JGV & Asociații’s brand—proving that legal environments can be both elegant and creatively bold.
