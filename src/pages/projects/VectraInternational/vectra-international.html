VECTRA INTERNATIONAL

Vectra International Furnishing a logistics centre in Codlea

SCOPE
Client

Vectra International · Logistics

Timeline

Completed August 2022

Area

1,000 m²

Location

Drumul Național 112, Codlea, Brașov

Vectra needed a larger space as its logistics business and transport routes expanded. The centre had to accommodate areas for administration, hosting visitors, dining and rest alongside warehousing and distribution.

JB Architecture designed the interiors. The furniture selection took account of internal circulation, daylight and the relationship between finishes.

THE OUTCOME

Workspace Studio supplied standard office furniture. The storage pieces were selected to fit the layout and colour palette.

THE DIFFERENTIATION

The work areas use minimalist furniture with slim dark frames against turquoise and anthracite tones. The executive meeting and negotiation areas use a darker palette with black details and wood finishes.
