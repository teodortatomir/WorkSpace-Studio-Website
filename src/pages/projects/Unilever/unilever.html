UNILEVER SOUTH CENTRAL EUROPE

Unilever South Central Europe A smaller headquarters for hybrid work

SCOPE
Timeline

Design and build services requested from 2021 · Completed October 2022

Area

1,200 m²

Location

AFI Lakeview, eighth floor, Bucharest

Unilever was moving its headquarters from Băneasa Business Center to AFI Lakeview. The company wanted a smaller office for hybrid work and a setting that would encourage employees to return after the pandemic.

The eighth floor was divided into two parts. The first combines open workstations with collaboration areas. The second is dedicated to collaboration and company events.

THE OUTCOME

The turnkey package included furniture, flooring, architectural works and building services. Workspace Studio also designed and fitted out the terrace connected to the collaboration and events area.

THE DIFFERENTIATION

Shared areas use Unilever’s corporate colours, graphics, soft seating, architectural cladding and acoustic ceilings. The cafeteria and meeting rooms draw on individual Unilever brands. Open ceilings, moss cladding, planters and wood help tie the interior together.
