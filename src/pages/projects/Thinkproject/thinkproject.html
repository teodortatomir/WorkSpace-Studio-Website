THINKPROJECT ROMANIA

Thinkproject Romania Design and fit-out at SkyTower

SCOPE
Timeline

Completed October 2026

Area

1,000 m²

Location

SkyTower, 28th floor, Bucharest

The project covered a 1,000 m² interior for Thinkproject Romania on the 28th floor of SkyTower.

Workspace Studio led the architecture and acted as general contractor. Its turnkey package covered the fit-out works, flooring, standard and custom-made furniture, and acoustic and architectural solutions.

THE OUTCOME

The project delivered fit-out works, flooring, and standard and custom-made furniture.

THE DIFFERENTIATION

The turnkey scope also included acoustic and architectural solutions.
