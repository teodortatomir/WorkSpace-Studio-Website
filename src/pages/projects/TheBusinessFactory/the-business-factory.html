Key Constraint

Expanding the coworking space rapidly while maintaining flexibility, comfort, and long-term durability for intensive daily use within a highly constrained budget.

The project was developed during the rapid growth of hybrid and remote work, when users increasingly wanted flexible workplaces closer to home. The challenge was to create a coworking environment that felt more welcoming and community-oriented than a traditional office, while still remaining highly functional and scalable.

The initial success of the project led to rapid occupancy and the need to double the workspace area without losing the original atmosphere and operational efficiency.

The workspace was designed as a hospitality-driven environment that blurs the line between work and social interaction. Open collaborative zones, private offices, dedicated desks, and informal lounges were organized around a naturally lit central space defined by vaulted wood ceilings and skylights.

Warm neutral tones, rustic textures, and subtle modern influences created a professional but relaxed atmosphere. Flexibility, durability, and acoustic comfort became essential design drivers due to the intensive coworking use.

The specification strategy focused on products suited for long-term high-traffic performance:

Herman Miller furniture systems were selected for their ergonomic performance, flexibility, and 12-year warranty — ideal for coworking environments with continuous use.

Mohawk Group carpet tiles provided high durability, acoustic comfort, and easy maintenance, making them highly suitable for intensive circulation areas.

Unilin PVC flooring was integrated in selected zones for durability, maintenance efficiency, and long-term operational performance.

Rather than maximizing desk density, the project focused on creating a comfortable and adaptable workplace ecosystem capable of evolving alongside its users.

THE OUTCOME
  • Utilization / Adoption: Strong demand led to the expansion and doubling of the coworking surface to approximately 2,200 sqm and over 250 workspaces.
  • Timeline / Budget Performance: Phase 1 – 2020: implementation completed in 3 weeks including furniture, flooring, and branding integration. Phase 2 – completed in April 2022: implementation completed in 4 weeks including furniture, flooring, and branding integration. The project was delivered within a highly optimized budget of approximately €300–350/sqm while maintaining commercial-grade workplace standards and long-term durability.
  • Client Feedback: The success of Phase 1 and the client’s exceptional feedback directly led to the expansion and implementation of Phase 2, while maintaining the original design identity and operational functionality.
  • Long-Term Impact: The Business Factory continues to operate as one of Brașov’s most active coworking hubs, validating the long-term demand for flexible, community-oriented workplace environments.
THE DIFFERENTIATION

Most coworking spaces prioritize density and short-term operational efficiency. Our approach focused on long-term usability, comfort, and community experience.

By combining hospitality-inspired design, durable commercial-grade solutions, strong acoustic performance, and flexible work typologies, the project became more than a coworking office — it became a workplace designed around human interaction, adaptability, and long-term daily use.
