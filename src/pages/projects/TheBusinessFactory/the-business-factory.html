The Business Factory – Coresi Business Campus

The Business Factory by CeetrusExpanding a coworking hub in two phases

SCOPE
Timeline

Phase 2 completed April 2022

Budget

More than €1.3 million in total investment in the coworking hub

Area

Approximately 3,000 m² across both phases

Location

H1, ground floor, Coresi Business Campus, 5 Turnului Street, Brașov

The coworking hub launched by Ceetrus filled quickly. Cartier Coresi expanded it to make more office space available.

Assembled Architecture & Design led the interior architecture. Allspace Interiors, a division of Workspace Studio, provided furniture, flooring and acoustic solutions.

THE OUTCOME

The 2022 expansion doubled the office area and brought the hub to more than 250 workspaces.

THE DIFFERENTIATION

The hub offers office spaces of different sizes and shared equipment for freelancers, entrepreneurs, company employees and other professionals.
