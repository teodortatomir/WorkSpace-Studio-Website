TCHIBO BRANDS

Tchibo Brands Turnkey interior at Business Garden

SCOPE
Timeline

Completed June 2025

Area

1,000 m²

Location

Business Garden Bucharest, eighth floor

The project concerned the Tchibo Brands interior on the eighth floor of Business Garden Bucharest.

Workspace Studio provided the interior design and general contracting. The turnkey scope comprised standard and custom-made furniture, flooring, and acoustic and architectural solutions.

THE OUTCOME

The completed project included flooring and both standard and custom-made furniture.

THE DIFFERENTIATION

The turnkey package brought acoustic and architectural solutions into the same scope as the furnishing.
