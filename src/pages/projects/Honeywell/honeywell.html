HONEYWELL

Honeywell Refurbishing four floors in two phases

SCOPE
Timeline

December 2023 to January 2025

Area

16,000 m²

Location

3 George Constantinescu Street, District 2, Bucharest

Honeywell needed to refurbish offices on the first, fourth and fifth floors and storage and archive space on the ground floor. The works were split into two phases to maintain business continuity.

With Infinity Design Office as architect, the turnkey refurbishment covered the fourth and fifth floors in the first phase and the first and ground floors in the second.

The project replaced all fan coil units, upgraded the electrical installations and lighting, and included architectural improvements. The furniture package included MillerKnoll Mirra 2 task chairs.

THE OUTCOME

All fan coil units were replaced. The project also upgraded the electrical installations and lighting and included architectural improvements.

THE DIFFERENTIATION

The turnkey refurbishment included MillerKnoll Mirra 2 task chairs alongside the building-services works.
