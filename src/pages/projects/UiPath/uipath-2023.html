UIPATH 2023

UiPath 2023 Technology-inspired office design

SCOPE
Timeline

Completed August 2023

Area

1,000 m²

Location

Bucharest

The office design was intended to reflect UiPath’s identity and give teams space to collaborate.

Workspace Studio designed and built the interior. Open, bright areas were planned to encourage interaction between teams.

THE OUTCOME

The project included standard and custom-made furniture, lighting, and acoustic and architectural solutions.

THE DIFFERENTIATION

Technology-inspired elements were paired with warm, organic accents. Herman Miller Aeron chairs were selected with employee comfort in mind.
