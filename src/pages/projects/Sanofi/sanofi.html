SANOFI ROMANIA

Sanofi Romania Turnkey fit-out at Equilibrium II

SCOPE
Timeline

Completed June 2026

Area

1,500 m²

Location

Equilibrium II, ninth floor, Bucharest

The interior fit-out was for Sanofi Romania’s premises on the ninth floor of Equilibrium II in Bucharest.

Gensler developed the concept design, and Proghaphic Architecture Studio was responsible for the architecture. Workspace Studio acted as general contractor for the interior fit-out.

The turnkey package included fit-out works, flooring, standard and custom-made furniture, and acoustic and architectural solutions.

THE OUTCOME

The turnkey package covered fit-out works, flooring, and standard and custom-made furniture.

THE DIFFERENTIATION
