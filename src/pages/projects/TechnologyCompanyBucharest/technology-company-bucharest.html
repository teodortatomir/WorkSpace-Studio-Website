Timeline

The project concerned a 1,000 m² office for a technology company in Bucharest.

Workspace Studio was responsible for the interior design and fit-out.

THE OUTCOME

The completed package included standard and custom-made furniture and architectural solutions.

THE DIFFERENTIATION

The workstation specification pairs electrically adjustable sit–stand desks with MillerKnoll Mirra 2 chairs.
