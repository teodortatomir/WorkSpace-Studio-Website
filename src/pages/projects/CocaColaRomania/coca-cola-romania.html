COCA-COLA ROMANIA

Coca-Cola Romania Refurbishment with a custom carpet pattern

SCOPE
Timeline

Completed January 2019

Coca-Cola wanted a modern office that reflected its brand culture.

Working with BL + Associates, Workspace Studio carried out the turnkey refurbishment. The package included fit-out and building services, standard and custom-made furniture, decorative wall coverings, branding and signage.

THE OUTCOME

Herman Miller furniture equipped all workstations. The office also includes meeting rooms, quiet rooms, a gym and collaboration areas.

THE DIFFERENTIATION

Traditional patterns and geometric forms informed the flooring, partitions, reception desk and coffee point. Carpet shapes were individually cut on site to follow the pattern.
