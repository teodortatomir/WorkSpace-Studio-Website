B. BRAUN MEDICAL

B. Braun Medical Interior design and general contracting

SCOPE
Timeline

Completed June 2025

Area

500 m²

Location

Business Garden Bucharest, second floor

The project covered a 500 m² interior for B. Braun Medical on the second floor of Business Garden Bucharest.

Workspace Studio designed the interior and acted as general contractor. The turnkey package included standard and custom-made furniture, flooring, and acoustic and architectural solutions.

THE OUTCOME

The turnkey delivery included flooring and standard and custom-made furniture.

THE DIFFERENTIATION

Acoustic and architectural solutions were included in the same delivery package.
