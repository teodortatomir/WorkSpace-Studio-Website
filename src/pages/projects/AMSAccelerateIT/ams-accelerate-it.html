AMS Accelerate IT Four types of work setting within 250 m²

SCOPE
Client

AMS Accelerate IT · Recruitment

Timeline

Completed September 2023

Area

250 m²

Location

Bucharest

As its team grew, AMS Accelerate IT wanted an office that reflected its brand and supported a range of day-to-day activities.

Workspace Studio developed the concept and carried it through the fit-out, including standard and custom-made furniture and architectural solutions.

THE OUTCOME

The layout provides separate settings for individual work, meetings, collaboration and breaks. Flexible workstations and ergonomic MillerKnoll furniture were selected for the office.

THE DIFFERENTIATION

The design incorporates AMS colours, logo and messaging. Energy-efficient lighting, Impact Acoustic panels, sustainable materials and waste reduction measures also formed part of the project.
