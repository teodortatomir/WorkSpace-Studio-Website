Technology Company- How do you turn the need for acoustic comfort into a visually defining design feature?

SCOPE
Client

Technology Company, tech sector

Timeline

Completion - February 2023

Budget

Not publicly disclosed

Delivered

Impact Acoustic solutions integrated into the client’s office at Palas Campus Iași

Key constraint

Reducing ambient noise and improving acoustic comfort without compromising the visual character of the interior concept

In technology-driven workplaces, high activity levels and simultaneous team interactions can generate significant ambient noise. For this project, the challenge was to introduce solutions that genuinely improved acoustic comfort and employee well-being, while also strengthening the visual identity of the office rather than appearing as purely technical interventions.

Workspace Studio integrated 100% sustainable Impact Acoustic solutions, using them both to enhance acoustic performance and to create an elegant office atmosphere based on natural tones.

From a design perspective, the project incorporated acoustic ceiling baffles, suspended elements, and wall claddings, each serving both a functional and expressive role. The solutions were selected to help mitigate sound-related discomfort while also shaping a distinctive, high-quality visual language throughout the space.

Unlike conventional practice, where acoustics are often treated late in the process as a corrective measure, this project embedded them into the design logic from the beginning, making them part of the office’s architectural identity.

THE DIFFERENTIATION

A standard approach would have treated acoustics as a corrective layer added only to reduce noise discomfort. Workspace Studio used them as a design tool with dual value: functional and aesthetic. This distinction matters especially in technology workplaces, where comfort, sustainability, and spatial identity must coexist within the same environment.
