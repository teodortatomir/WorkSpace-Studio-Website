UIPATH EXPERIENCE LAB 2018

UiPath Experience Lab 2018 Modular furniture for the Experience Lab

SCOPE
Timeline

Completed December 2018

The project included furnishing the UiPath Experience Lab for testing robots, alongside office, meeting and visitor areas. In the wider partnership, UiPath selected Workspace Studio for the purchase of more than 600 Herman Miller Aeron chairs for its offices worldwide. The fully configured Aeron chairs carried a 12-year guarantee for use across three shifts per day.

Iulia Trifan was the architect. Workspace Studio supplied office and custom-made furniture, including solid wood and metal pieces.

THE OUTCOME

The furnishing package covered offices, meeting and conference rooms, reception, informal areas and the cafeteria. BAUX acoustic cladding was used in the conference room.

THE DIFFERENTIATION

The modular furniture can be reconfigured. The mobile rooms incorporate writable whiteboard walls and acoustic elements.
