THOUGHTWORKS ROMANIA

Thoughtworks Romania An office refresh after an acquisition

SCOPE
Timeline

Completed October 2021

Location

239 Calea Dorobanți, first floor, Bucharest

After acquiring Gemini Solutions in Romania, Thoughtworks wanted to refresh its existing office and create a setting for an inclusive community.

Workspace Studio led the architecture and turnkey design and build.

THE OUTCOME

The refurbishment of the existing office was completed in October 2021.

THE DIFFERENTIATION

The concept drew on Thoughtworks’ motto about taking on ambitious challenges, choosing new career paths and using technology for social change.
