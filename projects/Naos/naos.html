NAOS SKIN CARE ROMANIA A Calm, Human‑Centric Workspace for a Science‑Driven Brand

SCOPE
Client

Naos Skin Care Romania, dermatology & skincare, medium‑sized organization

Timeline

Completed June 2022

Budget

Not disclosed

Delivered

450 m² on the 2nd floor of The Mark, Bucharest

Key constraint

Creating a warm, brand‑aligned environment within a compact floorplate while ensuring functional flow for multiple teams

Naos needed a workspace that reflected its scientific yet human‑focused identity, but the available 450 m² footprint required careful zoning to avoid crowding and to support both collaboration and quiet work. The challenge was to translate a global skincare brand’s values—care, clarity, well‑being—into a spatial experience that worked operationally for a growing local team.

The design centered on a soft, wellness‑oriented aesthetic, using natural textures, controlled lighting, and a clean material palette to echo Naos’ dermatological heritage. The layout balanced open areas with enclosed meeting rooms, ensuring acoustic comfort and intuitive circulation despite the compact space.

Implementation was handled end‑to‑end by Workspace Studio, coordinating architecture, finishes, and furniture to maintain consistency and minimize disruption. Every design decision—zoning, workstation density, shared‑space placement—was optimized for comfort and brand coherence.

Unlike standard small‑office fitouts that prioritize density, this approach prioritized well‑being and brand expression, resulting in a space that feels calm, premium, and purpose‑built.

THE OUTCOME
  • High employee adoption of shared and collaborative areas
  • Delivered on schedule in June 2022
  • Positive feedback on the atmosphere and comfort
  • A long‑term workspace aligned with Naos’ identity and operational needs
THE DIFFERENTIATION

A typical competitor might have maximized workstation count or reused generic finishes. Instead, this project treated the 450 m² as an opportunity to craft a brand‑immersive environment, proving that even compact offices can deliver a premium, wellness‑driven experience when design and implementation are tightly integrated.
