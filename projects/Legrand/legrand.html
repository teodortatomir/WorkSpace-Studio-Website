LEGRAND

LEGRAND ROMANIA More Capacity. The Same Footprint.

SCOPE
Client

LEGRAND Romania
Manufacturing and business services
540 m² office space, 41 employees

Timeline

2 months
Week 1–2: design & planning
Week 3–4: procurement & ordering
Week 5–6: phased decommissioning
Week 7–8: installation & fit-out
Week 9: handover

Budget

€50,000

Delivered

Office refurbishment within existing layout constraints
Minimal renovation including repainting works, acoustic elements, and furniture replacement
Area: 540 m²

Key constraints

A fixed budget framework and included the coordination of disposal and removal of existing furniture. The office remained fully operational during execution, requiring phased decommissioning and reorganization by department during working hours

The client needed to increase the number of workstations within the same existing office space, without expanding the footprint, performing structural demolition, or incurring additional costs. At the same time, there was a clear requirement to modernize the image of the workplace to better reflect the company’s current identity and standards.

The existing layout was not optimized for higher occupancy, limiting the efficient use of space and reducing functional flexibility. The workspace no longer reflected a contemporary working environment and required reconfiguration to support higher density while improving both usability and perception.

We developed a new workspace concept aligned with a refreshed vision for the office, focusing on increasing capacity while maintaining functionality and operational flow within the existing footprint. The redesigned layout enabled the integration of additional workstations without expanding the space or requiring structural interventions.

Key decisions were driven by an efficient space-planning strategy, where each area was redefined to support departmental structure and daily operations. Workstations were redistributed and resized to optimize density and circulation, while ensuring ergonomic and functional standards. Acoustic improvements and updated furniture solutions were integrated to enhance comfort in a higher-density environment.

Unlike standard fit-out approaches that rely on demolition or major spatial restructuring, this project was executed entirely within the existing structure. The solution was implemented in phases during working hours, allowing full occupancy to be maintained throughout the works while ensuring continuous coordination between departments.

THE OUTCOME
  • The project delivered a reconfigured and modernized workspace that successfully increased workstation capacity within the same 540 m² footprint, while maintaining full operational continuity.
  • The new layout significantly improved spatial efficiency and departmental organization, enabling better use of the available area and enhancing overall workplace functionality. Ergonomic furniture including electric height-adjustable desks, acoustic treatments, and a more contemporary design approach contributed to improved comfort and usability.
  • Client feedback:
    Workspace Studio demonstrated a strong understanding of our requirements and successfully identified the most suitable solutions for our needs. The team supported us throughout the entire process by proposing ergonomic furniture, a more vibrant and contemporary design approach, as well as effective acoustic solutions that significantly improved the comfort and functionality of our workspace. The client appreciated the professionalism, clear and consistent communication, and responsiveness during all project stages. Workspace Studio respected the agreed deadlines and carefully coordinated the installation process according to our departmental requirements, ensuring minimal disruption to daily operations.
  • Long-term impact:
    The refurbished workspace continues to support increased operational density while maintaining comfort and functionality. The improved layout and acoustic performance sustain day-to-day efficiency and reinforce a modern workplace image aligned with the company’s current standards.
THE DIFFERENTIATION

Rather than relying on conventional office redesign methods that prioritize structural change or extended downtime, this project was delivered entirely within an occupied workspace and strict operational constraints.

The approach focused on precision planning, phased execution, and coordination across departments, ensuring that transformation could happen without interrupting daily business activity. This allowed the client to achieve increased capacity and improved workplace quality simultaneously — without relocation, downtime, or additional structural cost.
