SIGNIVIS 2018

SIGNIVIS A Compact, High-Energy Workspace for an Ambitious SEO Company

SCOPE
Client

Signivis, SEO & digital marketing, small but fast-growing company

Timeline

Finalized September 2018

Budget

Not disclosed

Delivered

Full office furnishing with ergonomic and acoustic solutions

Key constraint

Creating a functional, comfortable environment within a small footprint while supporting a high-focus digital workflow

Signivis, a young and ambitious SEO company from Iași, needed a workspace that could support deep analytical work while also giving the team a sense of identity and comfort. The challenge was to design a compact office that avoided the typical “startup minimalism” and instead delivered real ergonomic value, acoustic control, and a professional atmosphere that could scale with the company’s ambitions.

The design centered on ergonomics and acoustic performance, essential for a team working long hours with data, content, and client communication. Workspace Studio equipped the office with Herman Miller, Gaber, BuzziSpace, and Baux acoustic panels—a premium mix rarely found in small-company fitouts.

The furniture and acoustic treatments were selected to:

  • Improve focus and reduce noise
  • Support healthy posture and long-term comfort
  • Create a clean, modern aesthetic aligned with a digital-first brand

This approach elevated the workspace far beyond the typical small-office setup, giving Signivis a professional environment that matched its ambitions.

THE OUTCOME
  • Noticeably improved acoustic comfort and focus
  • A workspace that supports both productivity and team identity
  • A high-quality environment delivered efficiently and tailored to a growing digital team
  • Long-term durability thanks to premium ergonomic and acoustic products
THE DIFFERENTIATION

Where many competitors would have proposed budget furniture and minimal acoustic treatment, this project delivered enterprise-grade ergonomics and sound control in a small-office context. The result is a workspace that empowers a young SEO company to operate with the comfort and professionalism of a much larger organization.
