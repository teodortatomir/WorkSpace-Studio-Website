EXIGER 2019

EXIGER DILIGENCE TECH A Precision-Built Workspace for a Precision-Driven Company

SCOPE
Client

Exiger Diligence Tech, global risk & compliance technology firm, mid-sized

Timeline

Completed February 2019

Budget

Not disclosed

Delivered

Full turnkey project including construction, installations, office furniture, custom furniture, signage, and wall cladding

Key constraint

Delivering a fully integrated, ready-to-operate workspace with zero tolerance for execution errors

Exiger needed a workspace that matched the rigor and precision of its global compliance and diligence operations. The challenge was to create an environment that supported high-focus analytical work while also enabling seamless collaboration between multidisciplinary teams. The project demanded a turnkey delivery, meaning every detail—from construction to custom furniture—had to be executed flawlessly and delivered as a unified whole.

The architectural vision, developed by Cumulus Architecture, centered on clarity, structure, and operational efficiency. Workspace Studio translated this vision into a complete, end-to-end implementation: construction works, technical installations, ergonomic office furniture, bespoke joinery, and branded signage were all coordinated under a single integrated workflow.

This approach ensured perfect alignment between design intent and execution. Every material, every junction, every custom element was calibrated to support Exiger’s culture of precision. The result is a workspace where functionality and identity reinforce each other—an environment built not just to look right, but to perform right.

Unlike standard fitouts that rely on fragmented suppliers, this project was delivered as a true turnkey solution, eliminating inconsistencies and ensuring a seamless, high-quality outcome.

THE OUTCOME
  • A fully operational workspace delivered as a complete, integrated package
  • High functional performance thanks to coordinated construction, installations, and custom elements
  • Strong client satisfaction due to the precision and reliability of the delivery
  • A long-lasting interior that continues to support Exiger’s analytical and collaborative workflows
THE DIFFERENTIATION

Where a typical competitor might have split responsibilities across multiple contractors—risking misalignment and delays—Workspace Studio delivered a single-source, precision-controlled turnkey project. This ensured consistency, speed, and a level of execution quality that mirrors Exiger’s own standards of diligence and accuracy.
