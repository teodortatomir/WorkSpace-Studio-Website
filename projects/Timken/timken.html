TIMKEN 2017

TIMKEN PWP ROMANIA Furnishing the First Greenfield Investment of a Global Industrial Leader

SCOPE
Client

Timken PWP, American global producer of bearings and power transmission components

Industry

Heavy industry / precision engineering

Timeline

Finalized February 2017

Budget

Not disclosed

Delivered

Design + office furniture, custom furniture, signage, wall cladding

Key constraint

Creating a fully functional, ergonomic office environment for a new manufacturing facility built from the ground up

Timken’s first greenfield investment in Romania required more than simply furnishing an office—it demanded a workspace that could support the operational rigor of a precision-engineering manufacturer entering a new market. The challenge was to design and equip an environment that matched Timken’s global standards of quality, durability, and efficiency, while ensuring the space could scale with future production and administrative growth.

Workspace Studio collaborated with arh. Delia A. Didea to develop a clean, functional, and robust office environment aligned with Timken’s industrial DNA. The solution combined:

  • Ergonomic office furniture for administrative and engineering teams
  • Custom furniture tailored to operational workflows
  • Signage and wall cladding that reinforced clarity, orientation, and brand presence

The design emphasized durability, simplicity, and efficiency—qualities essential for a manufacturing-adjacent workspace. Every element was selected to withstand intensive daily use while maintaining a professional, cohesive aesthetic.

Unlike standard office fitouts that prioritize visual impact, this project focused on operational reliability, ensuring that the workspace supported both precision work and day-to-day coordination with the production floor.

THE OUTCOME
  • A fully functional, ergonomic office environment delivered on schedule
  • A workspace aligned with Timken’s global standards of quality and operational discipline
  • Long-term durability thanks to custom and high-performance materials
  • A professional setting that supported the successful launch of Timken’s Romanian operations
THE DIFFERENTIATION

Where a typical competitor might have delivered a generic administrative office, Workspace Studio created a purpose-built environment tailored to the needs of a precision-engineering manufacturer. The integration of custom furniture, signage, and durable finishes ensured a workspace that not only looked right but performed reliably—mirroring Timken’s own commitment to engineering excellence.
